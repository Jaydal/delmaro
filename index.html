Engineering Process & Ownership FAQ

Do we own the full source code, database, and domain?
Yes. Every custom DELMARO build transfers repository access, database ownership, and deployment documentation directly to you—zero proprietary vendor lock-in.
How do we start if our workflows are still on paper or Excel?
Share screenshots or sample sheets in Messenger. Within 48 hours we map the data model and show a clickable scope plan before full development begins.
Can IoT hardware connect safely even with unstable site Wi-Fi?
Yes. Edge controllers buffer readings locally when Wi-Fi drops and sync encrypted payloads via MQTT or HTTPS automatically once connection restores.
What ongoing support or retainers are available after launch?
We offer optional monthly feature iterations, cloud monitoring, hardware replacement firmware, and staff onboarding as your branch count grows.
