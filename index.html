Common Questions From Business Owners

Can we see a sample before paying for a full system?
Yes. Once you message us what your business needs, we map out your workflow and show a clickable sample or draft layout first so you know exactly what you are getting.
How is a ₱500 website different from a custom system?
Our ₱500 Starter Website gives small businesses an instant online presence (services, prices, photos, map pin, and Messenger contact button). Custom systems add logins, live inventory tracking, online booking, automated billing, or hardware sensors tailored to your daily operations.
Are there forced monthly per-staff fees?
No per-seat lock-in fees on custom DELMARO builds—you own the finished website or system outright. Only optional custom domain renewals (.com/.ph), server hosting if needed for heavy systems, or SMS load apply if you request them.
What if our records are still in notebooks or messy Excel files?
No problem at all. Send photos of your current forms or spreadsheets in Messenger—we organize them cleanly, build a simple screen your staff can learn in minutes, and guide your team step by step.
